GSTR-2B Reconciliation Agent

Compares the purchase ledger against GSTR-2B filing data.

Before

Manual line-by-line comparison across two spreadsheets. Industry benchmark: about 40 hours per entity.

After

Exact-match comparison with AI classification. It found all 4 planted error types: a duplicate entry, a timing difference, an amount mismatch and an invoice missing from the books.

Impact

Catches what a manual review tends to miss, such as a ₹40 discrepancy on a single invoice.

In practice: what you give, receive and do

You give
Drop the Tally purchase ledger and the GSTR-2B download into two shared sheets (same layout every month).
You receive
An email listing every mismatch, each labelled duplicate entry, timing difference, amount mismatch or missing in books, with a one-line suggested action.
You do
Review the flagged invoices and correct them in Tally. Matching invoices need no action.
How often
Monthly, or whenever you upload new data.

Fixed along the way: a column-name collision that silently overwrote one dataset with the other, and a missing JSON.stringify that sent the AI a broken placeholder instead of real data.

Accounts Payable Invoice Pipeline

Reads PDF and image invoices and checks mandatory fields.

Before

Manual data entry from each vendor invoice. Industry benchmark: about 10 hours per week.

After

Automatic extraction with a validation check. Invoices missing a GSTIN, invoice number or total are blocked from posting and trigger an automatic warning email.

Impact

A sales invoice was once fed in where a purchase invoice belonged. The extraction was accurate but the document was wrong, so a validation rule now catches this.

In practice: what you give, receive and do

You give
Save the vendor invoice (PDF or image) into the 'AP Invoices' Drive folder.
You receive
A new row in the 'AP Draft Entries' sheet with vendor, invoice number, GSTIN, amounts and due date, marked 'Ready for Tally entry'. If the GSTIN, invoice number or total is missing, a warning email instead.
You do
Check the draft row and post it in Tally. For warnings, get the missing detail from the vendor.
How often
Whenever a new invoice arrives.

Fixed along the way: a .docx test file that AI vision cannot read (converted to PDF), and GSTIN truncation that became a permanent 15-character rule.

Automated MIS Generation

Pulls monthly P&L and drafts board-ready commentary.

Before

Commentary written by hand every month. Industry benchmark: about 2 days.

After

Scheduled generation that identifies margin compression, names likely causes and flags forward risk. It arrives as a draft emailed to the owner and is not sent to anyone else automatically.

Impact

A filter bug once pulled the wrong month, and the AI filled the gap with a believable but invented story. Every output is now checked against known figures.

In practice: what you give, receive and do

You give
Keep the monthly P&L figures sheet updated (current and previous month).
You receive
A DRAFT management commentary emailed to you, the owner, on the set date. It is not sent to anyone else automatically.
You do
Read it, edit if needed, then forward it to partners or the board.
How often
Monthly.

Fixed along the way: a case-sensitive sheet filter that returned zero rows, then the wrong month, and currency that defaulted to USD instead of ₹ with lakh-style grouping.

Compliance Calendar Agent

Alerts you when a GST, TDS or ROC deadline is within 7 days.

Before

Manual deadline tracking, prone to missed filings and late fees.

After

A daily check against a filing calendar. It fires only inside the warning window and stays silent otherwise. Tested in both directions.

Impact

A missed deadline carries direct penalties. This removes the manual tracking entirely.

In practice: what you give, receive and do

You give
Fill the filing calendar once (filing name, due date, form, status) and mark filings Done.
You receive
An email alert only when a deadline is within 7 days. No email means nothing is due soon.
You do
File the return and update the status in the sheet.
How often
A daily check, silent unless something is due.

Fixed along the way: silent failures parsing the DD-Mon-YYYY dates used in Indian filings, a zero-indexed month error and two field-name casing mismatches.

Bank Reconciliation Agent

Matches ledger invoices to bank payments by amount and date.

Before

Manual statement-to-ledger matching every week.

After

Each invoice is marked Paid, Unpaid or Check Manually (an automatic flag for ambiguous matches). It caught a ₹100 short payment and a genuinely unpaid invoice.

Impact

When two invoices share the same amount, it automatically flags them as Check Manually instead of guessing which payment belongs to which.

In practice: what you give, receive and do

You give
Upload the bank statement and the purchase ledger to the shared sheets.
You receive
An email listing only invoices that are Unpaid or Check Manually, with a suggested action. Short payments (for example ₹100 less than invoiced) are flagged.
You do
Chase unpaid invoices and manually confirm the Check Manually ones (for example two invoices with the same amount).
How often
Weekly.

Fixed along the way: a race condition between parallel branches (solved with a Merge node) and an execution-mode mismatch that duplicated rows 12 times.

Receivables Follow-Up Agent

Ages unpaid invoices and escalates the reminder tone in 3 stages.

Before

Manual tracking and hand-written follow-ups, with the tone depending on who wrote them that day.

After

Reminders in three escalating tones: gentle (up to 14 days overdue), firm (up to 44 days) and a final notice beyond that. Verified on test invoices at 5, 42 and 82 days overdue. Recommended setup: reminders come to you for approval first, not straight to your customers.

Known limitation

It re-sends a reminder every day it runs, because it does not yet track what was already sent. That is the next iteration, and it is documented rather than hidden.

In practice: what you give, receive and do

You give
Keep the receivables sheet current (customer, invoice, due date, amount, status Paid or Unpaid).
You receive
Reminder emails in three escalating tones: gentle (up to 14 days overdue), firm (up to 44) and a final notice beyond that. Recommended: they come to you for approval first, not straight to your customers.
You do
Approve or edit the reminder, and mark invoices Paid when payment arrives.
How often
Daily, once the 'already reminded' fix is in.

Fixed along the way: a Switch node with overlapping thresholds, replaced by a pre-computed stage value for unambiguous routing.

Payable Approval Agent

3-way match across Purchase Order, Goods Receipt and Invoice.

Before

Manual cross-checking of three documents, if it was done at all.

After

Matching orders are marked Approved. A deliberate mismatch (invoice for 50 units, only 45 received) triggered a Payment Hold email stating the exact reason. A person then decides whether it is a data-entry error or a real shortfall.

Impact

Stops payment for goods that were never delivered, a control small teams rarely have time to run by hand.

In practice: what you give, receive and do

You give
Fill the Purchase Order, Goods Receipt and Vendor Invoice sheets.
You receive
For each order, either Approved, or a Payment Hold email stating the exact reason (for example, invoice bills 50 units, only 45 received).
You do
Approved: proceed to pay. Hold: a person decides whether it is a data-entry error or a real shortfall. This is a deliberate human gate.
How often
Whenever a new invoice is entered.

Fixed along the way: a stray quote character in a node name that broke cross-node references, and an IF-branch wiring error that sent the approval email for every invoice.

Cash-Flow Forecast Agent

A 13-week (90-day) rolling forecast of your cash position.

Before

No routine forecast existed.

After

A 13-week rolling forecast email built from receivables (adjusted for real collection lag), approved payables and fixed costs, with plain-language commentary and the tightest week highlighted. It flagged a projected shortfall of -₹8,50,500 in week 9. If upstream checks show unresolved issues, you get a Forecast Blocked notice instead.

Impact

It will not run if reconciliation or payables approval still has unresolved issues, because a forecast on unverified numbers misleads.

In practice: what you give, receive and do

You give
Maintain the Receivables, Payables Summary (approved payables) and Recurring Costs sheets.
You receive
A 13-week rolling forecast email with plain-language commentary and the tightest week highlighted. If upstream checks show unresolved issues, a 'Forecast Blocked' notice instead.
You do
Act on any projected shortfall. On a Blocked notice, clear the flagged issues first and the forecast will run.
How often
Weekly.

Fixed along the way: comma-formatted amounts in Google Sheets read as text, which silently broke the calculation, and a cross-workflow race condition that read stale status data.

How the agents work together

The month-close sequence (reconciliation, payables approval, receivables follow-up and management commentary) ran in about 15 minutes on a live test.

  1. Reconcile

    GSTR-2B data is compared against the purchase ledger.

  2. Match payables

    A 3-way match checks the Purchase Order, Goods Receipt and Invoice.

  3. Chase receivables

    Overdue invoices get reminders that escalate in three stages.

  4. Forecast cash

    A 90-day forecast, built only on resolved reconciliation and payables data.

  5. Report

    A draft commentary is generated from the P&L and emailed to the owner.
